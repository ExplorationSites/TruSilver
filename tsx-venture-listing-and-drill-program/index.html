The program

Five holes, 3,500 metres, and the permit’s already in place.

Our Sturgis-Walton Silver Project sits in Hants County, about 70 kilometres northwest of Halifax.5 The permit for the five holes runs to March 2028,6 and we’re currently negotiating the contract with the driller, right now.2

The target sits at depth, beside the historic Walton Mine, on ground we own 100%.3, 5 No NSR exists on the property.5 An NSR is a royalty on what a mine sells. Nobody holds one here, so nothing that comes out of this ground has a slice owed to someone else.

We’ve drilled here before. In 2023 we put down a 557-metre hole that cut two mineralized zones carrying silver and copper.5 The next five holes test the target at depth.3 The first is designed to cut both target zones from the west.7

The question the five holes are designed to answer is a plain one. Our 2021 magnetic survey and our 2024 gravity and AMT surveys define one large, deep anomaly beside the historic Walton Mine, which we read as the trunk of the system that fed it.7 The next five holes will test that interpretation.

If you want the thinking behind the target, here’s the branch-and-trunk theory behind our drill target.

Silver and Nova Scotia

Silver’s short, and Nova Scotia’s saying yes.

The world used 1.13 billion ounces of silver in 2025. Mines produced 846.6 million. It was the fifth straight year the market ran a deficit.55 We’ve put together another report giving you more on why most of the world’s silver comes from by-product mines.

Silver joined the US list of critical minerals in November 2025.56 Three months later the US Export-Import Bank put a US$10 billion loan behind a national critical minerals reserve, alongside nearly US$2 billion of private money.57

Nova Scotia added silver to its own critical minerals list in May 2025.58 In July 2026 the province approved the Scotia Mine to restart, the first critical mineral mining project approved since its Critical Minerals Strategy came out. The Minister’s words: “There is momentum that is undeniable.”59

Rio Tinto has staked claims surrounding our project.3 Here’s why Rio Tinto’s claim staking matters to our Nova Scotia story.

Atlantic Gold was a TSX Venture company when it built the Moose River gold mine in Nova Scotia.60 It declared commercial production in March 2018.61 St Barbara, the company that bought it, has already raised its Nova Scotia exploration budget 64% for its 2027 financial year.62

Silver’s short. Nova Scotia’s open for mining again. Rio Tinto has staked the ground around us. That’s where our five holes go in, and the results come to your inbox the day they’re public.

Frequently asked

What is TruSilver Metals?

TruSilver Metals is a Canadian mineral explorer focused on our 100%-owned Sturgis-Walton Silver Project in Nova Scotia. We’re preparing five diamond drill holes totalling 3,500 metres to test a deep target near the historic Walton Mine.3

Is TruSilver Metals publicly traded?

Yes. TruSilver Metals trades on the TSX Venture Exchange under the symbol TAG, which most quote services list as TAG.V, following our qualifying transaction with Galaxy Ventures Inc. You can follow our exploration updates and publicly released drill results through this page.

What is TruSilver drilling, and when does it start?

TruSilver Metals is mobilizing for a five-hole, 3,500-metre diamond drilling program at Sturgis-Walton. We’ll update this page as soon as the first hole is under way.

When will the drill results be reported?

TruSilver Metals will share the drill assays once we’ve received and checked the results and released them publicly. We’ll report them interval by interval as the lab returns them, rather than holding them for a whole hole. Laboratory turnaround is currently four weeks or more. Every result goes out by news release, and to the email list on this page as soon as it’s public.7

What are the five holes meant to test?

TruSilver Metals is testing a deep exploration target where our magnetic, gravity and AMT surveys point to the same part of the Sturgis-Walton property.7 Our first hole is designed to cut both target zones from the west. Downhole surveys from each hole feed one 3D model to aim the next.7

How do I get TruSilver’s drill results?

Enter your email in the form on this page, and TruSilver Metals will send you drilling updates and assay results after they’re released publicly. You’ll be able to follow the five-hole program from its first milestones through the results.

How much has TruSilver raised, and did insiders invest?

TruSilver Metals raised about C$2,830,090 in the financing completed ahead of drilling. Directors and officers on both sides of the transaction subscribed for about C$545,000 of it: about C$305,000 from the TruSilver side and C$240,000 from the Galaxy Ventures side.3 Our October presentation reports 39% insider ownership and C$3.6 million raised since the start.7

Who leads exploration at TruSilver Metals?

Matt Rees, a Director of TruSilver Metals and the Qualified Person for this page, served as Chief Geologist at IAMGOLD. Our Project Geologist, Jeff Burke, coordinates field work, drilling, geological interpretation and the checks on our technical data.10

Who is Peter Hancock?

Peter Hancock is a director of TruSilver Metals and our Lead Director7. He runs Sherritt International, sits on the Sibanye-Stillwater board and led the completion and ramp-up of the US$7 billion Koniambo nickel project.

Does TruSilver have a mineral resource?

No mineral resource or mineral reserve has been defined at TruSilver Metals’ Sturgis-Walton Silver Project.6 Our five-hole program will test our exploration model and give our geologists fresh information about the rock at depth.

Which silver explorers started on the TSX Venture Exchange and were later bought?
